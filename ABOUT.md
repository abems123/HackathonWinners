# About Bron

**Bron shows payroll consultants which rule applies to a client, and whether that rule can still be trusted.**

## How to use it

1. **Open a client** under *My clients*.
2. **Pick a topic**, for example *Payroll input* or *Telework allowance*.
3. **Read the passages that apply.** Each one shows the exact text from its source document, and client-specific exceptions appear above the general rule.

## What the labels mean

- **Confirmed**: a named person checked this passage against the latest version of its source.
- **Needs review**: the source changed, expired or conflicts with another source. Don't rely on it yet.
- **Open question**: someone asked about it, and an expert has been assigned to answer.
- **Conflict**: a person confirmed that two sources disagree.

## Good to know

- **Found but not applicable** lists rules that exist but don't fit this client, for example a Dutch rule for a Belgian client.
- **No answer** means there is no trusted source for this question. Bron then names the expert to ask.
- **Review queue**: when a source owner uploads a new version, only the passages that actually changed go back to the queue for review.
- **Only people can confirm or resolve.** The AI check can only point out possible conflicts.
- **Every decision needs a reason** and is recorded in the *Activity log*.
