# Telework allowance · PC 200

The monthly telework allowance is EUR 154.20.

Employees must work from home on a regular basis.

A written telework agreement is required.

The allowance covers heating and electricity.

Office supplies are included in the allowance.

Equipment remains the property of the employer.

Requests are reviewed by the payroll consultant.

Keep the agreement in the employee file.

Review eligibility at the annual payroll check.
