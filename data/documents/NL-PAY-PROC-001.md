# Monthly payroll input procedure – Netherlands

## Purpose
This procedure describes how and when clients submit variable payroll input for Dutch payrolls.

## Deadline
Payroll input for a pay month must be submitted **no later than the 18th calendar day of that same month**. If the 18th falls on a weekend, the deadline moves to the preceding Friday.

## After the deadline
- Processing takes place between the 19th and the 22nd.
- Payslips are published on the 25th of the pay month.

## Exceptions
Deviations must be approved by the Payroll Process Owner NL.
