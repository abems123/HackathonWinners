# Monthly payroll input procedure – Belgium

## Purpose
This procedure describes how and when clients submit variable payroll input (worked hours, absences, overtime, bonuses) for Belgian payrolls.

## Deadline
Payroll input for a pay month must be submitted **no later than 17:00 on the 5th working day of the following month**. Example: input for September is due on the 5th working day of October.

Working days exclude weekends and Belgian public holidays. The exact date per month is listed in the Payroll calendar 2026 – Belgium (BE-PAY-CAL-2026).

## After the deadline
- Working days 6 to 8: payroll processing and checks.
- 10th working day: payslips are published (see BE-PAYSLIP-001).
- Input received after the deadline is processed in the next pay month, unless the payroll consultant approves a correction run.

## Exceptions
Client-specific deviations from this deadline must be approved by the Payroll Operations Lead and recorded in the client file. Deviations that are not recorded in the client file are not valid.

## Change log
- v4.2 (March 2026): deadline moved from the 3rd to the 5th working day, to give clients more time after month end.
