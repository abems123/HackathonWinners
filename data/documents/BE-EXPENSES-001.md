# Expense claims – Belgium

Employees submit expense claims (travel, meals, equipment) in the employee portal, with a photo of the receipt.

## Deadline
Claims must be submitted **by the 20th of the month following the expense**. Late claims are only accepted with manager approval.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid together with the next payroll.
