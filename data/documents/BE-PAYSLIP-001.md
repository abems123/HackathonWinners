# Payslip publication – Belgium

Payslips are published in the employee portal on the **10th working day of the month following the pay month**. Employees receive a notification by e-mail and in the app.

If a correction run is needed, the corrected payslip is published as a separate document. The original payslip stays visible.

The exact publication dates are listed in the Payroll calendar 2026 – Belgium (BE-PAY-CAL-2026).
