# Absence registration – Belgium

Employees or their managers register absences (holiday, sick leave, other leave) in mysdworx Time.

Absences must be **approved before the payroll input deadline** (see BE-PAY-PROC-001) to be included in that month's payroll. Absences approved later are processed in the next pay month.
