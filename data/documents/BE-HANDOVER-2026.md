# Client handover checklist v2

Use this checklist when you take over a client from a colleague.

1. Open the client's workspace in mysdworx and read the client file.
2. Review all open tickets for the client.
3. Check client-specific exceptions. These should be in the client file, but they are sometimes only agreed in Teams. Always check the client's Teams threads from the last three months.
4. Shadow one full payroll run with the previous consultant.
5. Introduce yourself to the client contact, together with the previous consultant.
6. Mark the handover as complete in the client workspace.
